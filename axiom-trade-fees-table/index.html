Axiom Trade fees table: ranks, cashback and 10% off

Last checked: 2 November 2026

Other costs

  • Solana network: a priority fee paid to validators, and an optional bribe setting (Solana fees page).
  • Perpetuals: through Hyperliquid, up to 50x leverage, with Axiom taking 0.01% per transaction (perpetuals page).

Who gets what from a referral

The new user gets 10% off. The person whose link was used earns 30% of Axiom's net fee on that user's trades, plus 3% and 2% from two further levels (referral page). That share is worked out from the fee Axiom actually collects after your discount, so it does not add to what you pay.
